2 · Fine tuning, Mii style

Move, size and tilt each feature

Every feature has a few integer sliders on top of its shape choice. Small integer steps keep the avatar code short (one byte per slider) and make two people with the same parts still look different. Changes here apply to the selected person across the whole page.

Expressions only swap lids, brows, mouth and cheeks. Transforms (zombie, festive) recolor and add props, but keep face shape, hair, headwear and glasses so the person stays recognizable.

What to decide next

Tell me what to keep

  • Which style, or which mix. Sticker proportions with cel shading is possible, since style is just a rendering layer over the same traits.
  • Whether the tuning ranges feel right: too subtle, too wild, or missing a slider (ear size, head width, mouth tilt).
  • Which expressions belong in the default set, and which transforms beyond zombie and festive you want first.
  • Anything that reads wrong or stereotyped for a person or headwear. Those parts get redrawn before the schema is locked.
